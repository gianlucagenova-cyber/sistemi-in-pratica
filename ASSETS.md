### 1. storia-computer

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: editorial illustration for an Italian high-school computing course website, landscape 3:2. Polished tactile 3D clay-and-paper illustration, sophisticated friendly classroom aesthetic, soft studio lighting, fine grain, deep forest teal #075f51, pale mint, warm ivory, small apricot accents. Subject: the evolution of personal computing, a charming cream vintage CRT computer with keyboard on the left, a modern slim desktop on the right, a small circuit board between them, connected by an elegant flowing ribbon. Ivory backdrop. Visual theme: history and fundamentals of operating systems.
